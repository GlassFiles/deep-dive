How the dive is scored

A round has up to five kinds of question. Each is worth up to 100 points, and a perfect round reaches full depth, 11,000 m, however many questions the pack sets.

Rare answer. Name something in a category. Answers sit in four tiers, Shallows, Reef, Wreck and Abyss: the most familiar score a little, the rarest a lot. Answers we do not list are judged by Claude when this page can ask it; otherwise they score a low "not on our list" mark.

Closest number. Estimate a number. The closer, the deeper; big numbers are judged by ratio, so 2x too high counts the same as 2x too low.

Bet on it. Pick an answer, then bet: Hunch (the default), Fairly sure or Certain. Right gains depth by your bet; wrong costs depth, and Certain costs most. Only say Certain when you are.

Put in order. Arrange 4 or 5 items. Every pair in the right order earns credit.

Mark scheme. Write a short answer; it is marked against key points, exam style, by Claude or by you.

Golf. The same scores, shown as drives: a perfect answer carries about 330 yards, a wrong "Certain" bet finds the pond and costs yards, and your round is the yards added up. Same questions, same order on the scoreboard.
